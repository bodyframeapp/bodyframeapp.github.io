Bodyframe

Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026 · Applies to the Bodyframe app for Android

In short

  • Everything you enter stays on your phone.
  • There is no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • We never see, collect, sell or share your data.

Who we are

Bodyframe is a personal tracker for weight, calories, steps, body measurements and progress photos. It is made by Bodyframe ("we"). You can reach us using the contact details at the end of this policy.

What the app stores

The app stores only what you type or choose yourself:

  • Measurements and entries: the metrics you create (for example weight, calories, steps, waist) and the values and times you log.
  • Profile details: units, sex, age, height and usual daily steps, used to estimate your maintenance calories.
  • Plan and charts: your calorie plan, its weekly adjustments and your chart settings.
  • Progress photos: photos you pick from your gallery, with the date, angle and note you give them. The app resizes each photo and keeps its own copy.

This information can relate to your health. It is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to access it.

No internet use

This version of Bodyframe works fully offline and does not request internet access. It does not load ads, analytics, crash reporting or any third-party services, and its fonts are built into the app.

Photos

When you add a photo, Android's own photo picker lets you choose which images to share with the app. Bodyframe cannot see the rest of your gallery. The copies it keeps stay inside the app and are not added back to your gallery or uploaded anywhere.

Backups and exports you make

Profile → Backup lets you export your data as a JSON or CSV file. Android's share sheet then asks where to send it, for example Google Drive, email or your Files app. That file goes only where you choose, and from then on it follows the privacy terms of that destination. The full backup includes your progress photos unless you untick “Include photo files”, so keep it somewhere private. The CSV holds only your entries.

If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include the app's data in your phone's backup to your Google account, under Google's terms. You can manage this in your phone's settings.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete single entries, metrics, photos or your plan inside the app at any time.
  • To delete everything, clear the app's storage in Android Settings → Apps → Bodyframe → Storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Uninstalling removes all data stored by the app. We cannot restore it, so keep a backup if you want one.

Children

Bodyframe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Health information

Estimates in the app, such as maintenance calories and weight projections, are for general guidance only and are not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or dietitian before making big changes to how you eat or train.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that handle data differently, for example syncing with health apps, subscriptions or accounts, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date at the top.
